# ExoRA

ExoRA (Exposome for Rheumatoid Arthritis) is an ontology for the semantic
representation of heterogeneous data collected in rheumatoid arthritis research,
with the PROMESS study as its initial application context.

The ontology aims to provide a coherent representation of participant-related
data coming from different parts of a clinical research study, including study
organization, questionnaire-based assessments, recorded responses, derived
scores, clinical information, medication-use records, biological samples,
exposure information, life events, location history, and temporal information.


## Domain reference material

The conceptualization has been developed from domain documentation associated
with the PROMESS study, including:

- the PROMESS clinical research protocol;
- the PROMESS Case Report Form (CRF);
- clinical questionnaires;
- psychological questionnaires;
- dietary and physical-activity questionnaires;
- validated psychological assessment instruments;
- the semi-structured research interview guide;
- documentation concerning projective assessments;
- the self-drawing protocol;
- documentation concerning PROMESS data organization and integration.

Some source documents may not be redistributed in this public repository until
their redistribution rights have been confirmed. In such cases, this repository
provides bibliographic or descriptive references rather than copies of the
documents.
